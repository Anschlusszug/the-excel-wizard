The problem

Sound familiar?

Every spreadsheet starts clean. Then it grows — one tab at a time, one "just a quick formula" at a time — until nobody trusts the numbers anymore.

  • Formulas that break the moment someone adds a row
  • Five versions of the same file, none of them authoritative
  • Manual copy-paste that quietly corrupts your data
  • Reports that take a full day to rebuild by hand
  • Nobody left who actually knows how it works

The cost isn't the hours. It's the decisions you make on numbers you can't verify.
